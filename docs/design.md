# Warhead tactics

Present tense. Deltas → `docs/changelog.md`.

## Product

Mobile web survivors run. The deck is the run's airdrop pool. The Lead steps off alone, and octacopter cargo drones bring the cards in. They can hold a path into a harder field, or reach the LZ, arm the flare, wait out the craft, and load one by one. An empty deck fields one Cell. The first Cell is the Lead. A section with no living unit loses. A hull or a sentry does not keep it. The player builds a counted deck (cap 12) and picks Light, Standard, or Heavy. The Cell is free. Every other researched card is paid in credits for that deployment. Taking the same card again, in one deck or on the next round, raises that price by its base for each step. A round that leaves the card out cools one step. Unknown cards stay hidden until a field find. A find fades in while it moves into place. A find must be researched, at twenty times its deploy price, before it can be bought. Credits come from kills, the Purge and Haul bounties, and salvage, intel, and samples. Extract banks that haul, pays 40 more, and pays back the deployment cost of each unit, vehicle, and fitted gear that boarded. Section gear pays back when someone boards. A wipe banks none. Cards airdrop in shuffled order, each a walk from the squad and from the last one, and the next waits. Pause, a roster, a nine-slot toolbar, and an end tally sit on the field. One campaign save, which can be restarted. A system menu sets thud volume and mute. A debug menu sets the field dither: levels, spread, scale, size, pattern, strength, cut, ink, blend, and whether it sits on the mud or the whole field. Opening the deck rolls a seed. No campaign map.

## World

Three Uluses already annihilated each other. Residue is rad, burn-chem, bio, and AI-oxide. The section can field Cells, Aegis, Forge, Wisp, an engineer, a relay, a recondog, a copter, a hunter-killer, a biohawk, a middlestrike, a cyberassassin, a cyberoperator, medic, jeep, bike, buggy, hoverbike, MRAP, HEX scout, HEX line, HEX siege, APC, plow, tank, truck, technical, SUV, steed, cart, mule, MLRS, IFV, MBT, an unmanned UGV, an unmanned leashed gunboat, a 60mm, drill, pump, and cutter. A rifle, MG, sniper, grenade, mortar, blade, or bow is gear. It fits a soldier and replaces the gun. The Lead starts with a rifle. A drop with no gun is issued one: a rifle for a soldier, a light mount for a hull or a follower. Androids keep their own gun. The first Cell is the Lead. Ferals and Rust fight the section and each other: shambler, doghead, pighead, goathead, crowhead, mutant, drone, walker, scoot, tract, coil, flake, roller, kite, slab, reach, mast, dray, rust plow, post, bloom, cluster, fog, ball, amalgam. Marsh bogs a hull. A hoverbike does not. A stash is a short hold that opens a find. Until a run has offered one, a stash may also turn up a named soldier. Extract keeps that soldier. Death removes a veteran, and redeploying one costs 80. Props are burnt trees, ruins, wire, wrecks, posts, and teeth. The field runs well past the LZ and the path. The camera sits wide. The field is dithered mud, burnt timber, and drab coats. Chrome is VGA grayscale. Color is rare: the title, the flare, dried blood, oxide, and a health bar that turns gold, then red.

## Loop

One move stick. Press and hold the left of the field to move. Hold Q or Space to group and E or Shift to spread. The pause menu lists the keys. Keys 1-9 fire the toolbar slots. Units auto-aim. Group closes the file in a glide, then boards one body per tap or hold-tick, alternating vehicles. The Lead takes the fastest hull. Spread disembarks one body before the file opens in a glide. The camera stays on the commander and eases well out as the file opens. Button labels show the next step. Bracket labels mark fire, boarding, finds, and loot. Red is a squad KIA or a lost part. An enemy down-label is gray and reads DOWN. An LZ arrow names the zone and shows the arm and craft counts. A gold column is the flare, and the craft, a large brick-shaped cargo VTOL, descends onto it. An inbound drop descends on its flare under an octacopter. A soldier or a hull hangs on the cables and is set down directly. Gear hangs as a crate. The empty drone climbs away. Salvage, intel, and samples sit on the ground. A relic is a find, and it pays no credits. Pickup is a short reach. A body that has not joined holds at the drop and shoots. Edge marks also point at empty hulls and stragglers. A corpse is that figure laid flat. A part is that figure's head, limb, wheel, or turret. A plate names it while it is in view. A dim line drifts in the quiet. Hits land as muffled distant thuds. Hits tear off a limb or a wheel and throw it. A turret stays on the hull until the hull is badly hurt. Dead enemies stay on the ground. Shots, blasts, and breaks leave splatters, burns, and craters. A joined unit stays on foot and boards after a short delay when everyone else is mounted and a seat is free. Driving off leaves bodies behind. A Cell waits out a visible deploy. Aegis and the APC shoot on the move. A fitted grenade or mortar lobs. Feral bites land on contact. A goathead shoots. A crowhead lobs gnats. A Rust gun throws a straight bolt that can miss. Flakes come in a pack. A coil bites. Crewed hulls steer, accelerate, brake, and reverse. A moving hull rams harder and shoves. A hull at rest takes no collision damage, and a bump does not stall it. A second crewed hull rides beside the lead, clear of its skin. A crewed hull shoves an empty one aside. The stick stays with the Lead. If the Lead is down, it passes to a soldier, or to a follower when no soldier is left. A body still outside after the craft is down is left behind. Vehicles take hull damage. The plow and the tank break props. A Forge and an engineer dig cover down. A medic cannot patch an android. A Wisp opens the section's sight. A living relay brings the craft sooner. A hoverbike ignores mud. A light hull takes the hit, then skirts the cover. Gear auto-fits: plate, scope, drum, bipod, stim, plating, ram. Section tools: frag, mines, sentry, kit, radio, nitro, CAS, NVG, thermal, thermite, EMP, smoke, stun, FPV. An exo frame fits a soldier. A jetpack ignores mud. A filter ignores weather. A litter lets the bearer patch. An axe chews cover. An AT blast punches armor. A living operator jams Rust. A recondog carries night sight. A copter and a hunter-killer follow the Lead and do not board. The gunboat is an unmanned leashed assault copter. The UGV follows and shoots with nobody aboard. Thermite ignores armor. EMP bites only Rust. Smoke hides the squad from ranged hostiles. Each field rolls a ground, a bearing for the path and the LZ, a pressure, and a sky pair. A deeper field rolls those again. Weather snaps between that pair. It slows the file and cuts sight. Night cuts sight further. A flesh soldier on foot throws a beam along his shot, and that beam sees at day range. A hostile in the beam shoots back at day range. Androids carry no light. NVG and thermal shut the beams, and daylight shuts them. A living android carries thermal. CAS is a gunship pass along a crowd.

Cost vs 2×:
  - ~4× pixel authorship (every row/HOLD/prop/vehicle/anim offset)
  - ASCII rows get long; bake cost and canvas memory grow
  - On mobile ÷3–4 buffer, 4× sprites at 1:1 dominate the frame (199)
  - At matched world height (200/203), 4× buys softer edges, separate gun parts,
    coat folds — but the field dither/mud grain stays coarse relative to the figure
  - Sweet spot from earlier RES-NOTES was ~24px; 4× (~64) is past that unless
    the whole buffer scale drops (e.g. ÷2 or 1:1 screen) so those pixels read

If applied with today's buffer: must draw at 1/4 scale or zoom out hard.
